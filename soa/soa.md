# Statement of Applicability (SoA)

## Purpose

The Statement of Applicability (SoA) records the information security controls considered necessary for the SecureSure Insurance Information Security Management System (ISMS), the justification for their inclusion, the implementation status of applicable controls, and the justification for excluding any Annex A controls.

## Framework

- Standard: ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- Control reference: Annex A
- Organization: Fictional SecureSure Insurance
- ISMS scope: As defined in `isms-scope/organizational-context-and-scope.md`

## SoA Management Approach

The SoA will be maintained in an Excel workbook covering all 93 Annex A controls.

For each control, the workbook will record:

- Control identifier and official title
- Control applicability decision
- Justification for inclusion or exclusion
- Related information security risk IDs, where relevant
- Proposed control owner
- Implementation status
- Evidence reference
- Assessment notes and required actions

## Applicability and Implementation

Applicability and implementation are separate decisions. A control may be applicable but not yet implemented.

Control applicability will be justified using the ISMS scope, risk assessment and treatment decisions, applicable legal and contractual requirements, and other relevant business requirements.

An Annex A control will not be marked implemented or effective solely because it appears in the risk-to-control mapping.

## Evidence and Governance

Control implementation and effectiveness claims must be supported by appropriate evidence. Until evidence is available, the workbook will clearly identify assumptions, planned activities, and unassessed controls.

Control owners, applicability decisions, exclusions, and implementation status are proposed for this simulated portfolio and require validation.

## Disclaimer

This is a fictional learning and portfolio project. It does not represent an actual ISO 27001 certification, completed audit, or verified assessment of an organization's security controls.
